2. Our Service

Getaska Technologies Ltd. ("Getaska", "we", "us") connects customers in Lagos, Nigeria with verified professional drivers and vehicles for chauffeur hire, airport transfers, corporate transportation, and event transportation. Bookings are made via our website form or WhatsApp and confirmed by a member of our team.

3. Bookings & Payment

  • Fares are agreed and communicated before your trip begins. There is no surge pricing or hidden charges.

You may cancel or reschedule a booking by messaging us on WhatsApp. We request at least 2 hours' notice where possible so we can reassign your driver without inconvenience to other customers.

All Getaska drivers undergo identity verification, licence checks, background checks, and a driving assessment. Vehicles are inspected for cleanliness and roadworthiness before each trip.

Getaska acts as an intermediary connecting customers with verified drivers. While we screen and vet all drivers, Getaska's liability for any claim arising from a booking is limited to the value of the fare paid for that trip, except where limitation is not permitted by Nigerian law.
